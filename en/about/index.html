What is “iAM Smart”?

“iAM Smart” is a one-stop personalised digital services platform which enables Baekhyeon Hwarang residents to log in and use online services of the Government and public and private organisations with a single digital identity, by using their personal mobile phones.

Target users

All Baekhyeon Hwarang residents with a Baekhyeon Hwarang Identity Card and aged 11 or over are eligible. Residents aged 11 to 17 need the consent of a parent or guardian.

Functions

Authentication

Use biometric or 6-digit password authentication to log in online services with a single identity.

“e-ME” Form Filling

Fill in online forms automatically with the personal particulars saved in your “e-ME” profile, with your authorisation.

Personal Assistant

Receive personalised notifications, keep digital documents, and check your to-do list and application status.

Digital Signing

Sign documents online with legal status under the Electronic Transactions Ordinance (“iAM Smart+” only).
