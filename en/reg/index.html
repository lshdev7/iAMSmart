Registration Methods

Mobile App (“iAM Smart+”) – Aged 18 or over

Please prepare: your Baekhyeon Hwarang Identity Card; a personal mobile phone with biometric authentication and the “iAM Smart” app; and an email address.

  1. 1

    Open the “iAM Smart” mobile app

  2. 2

    Select “Start Quick Registration”

  3. 3

    Select “Adult (Aged 18 or over)”

  4. 4

    Read and agree the “Declaration of the Applicant”

  5. 5

    Select “Start”

  6. 6

    Capture the front side of the identity card

  7. 7

    Read the chip of the identity card

  8. 8

    Facial identification and liveness detection

  9. 9

    Complete the phone’s built-in biometric authentication

  10. 10

    Set up a 6-digit “iAM Smart” password

  11. 11

    Provide an email address

  12. 12

    “iAM Smart+” registration is completed

Mobile App – Aged 11 to 17

The applicant should be accompanied by a parent or guardian who is an “iAM Smart” user. The parent or guardian logs in to his/her “iAM Smart” account and scans the QR code shown on the applicant’s phone to give consent. The remaining steps are the same as for adults.

Upgrade to “iAM Smart+” in the app

  1. 1

    Select “Upgrade to iAM Smart+”

  2. 2

    Agree the declaration

  3. 3

    Scan the identity card

  4. 4

    Facial identification

  5. 5

    “iAM Smart+” upgrade is completed

Self-registration kiosk / Registration service counter

  1. 1

    Select the language and “Registration of iAM Smart”

  2. 2

    Scan the kiosk QR code with the app

  3. 3

    Insert the identity card into the card slot

  4. 4

    Facial identification

  5. 5

    Biometric authentication on the phone

  6. 6

    Set up the 6-digit password and email address

  7. 7

    Registration is completed
