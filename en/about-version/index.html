“iAM Smart” Account Versions

“iAM Smart” account is available in two versions, namely “iAM Smart” and “iAM Smart+”.

Residents may register for “iAM Smart” or “iAM Smart+” per their needs. The difference between the two versions is that “iAM Smart+” will additionally provide digital signing and step-up authentication functions.

How to upgrade to “iAM Smart+”

Users of “iAM Smart” can upgrade to “iAM Smart+” in the mobile app by scanning the identity card and completing facial identification, or at a self-registration kiosk or registration service counter.
