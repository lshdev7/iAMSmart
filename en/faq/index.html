  • A2 No. Registration must be completed by the identity card holder himself/herself.

  • A3 Yes, you can still use the original login method of those online services.

Login and password

  • A1 The password is used when the phone’s biometric authentication is not available or fails. You can reset it with the “Reset Password” function in the app. You will receive a notification whenever the password is used to log in.

  • A2 You are not required to change it regularly, but we recommend changing it at a regular interval, for example every 90 days.

  • A3 If an incorrect password is entered ten times in a row, the account becomes invalid and you will be required to register again.

Lost mobile phone and switch account to new mobile phone

  • A1 Call the “iAM Smart” hotline, or use the account suspension function at a self-registration kiosk or registration service counter. To reactivate, register again with another mobile phone; the account on the old phone will be deleted.

  • A2 Use the “Transfer account to another mobile” function in the app on the existing phone, or rebind the account on the new phone. Upon success, the account on the original phone will be deleted.

  • A3 To de-register, use “De-register” under “Settings” > “Security & Privacy” in the app and confirm with biometric authentication or your password. Information saved in “e-ME” is kept for 12 months after suspension.

Personal Code

  • A1 It contains your partial English name (e.g. CHOI Y** N**), age group (11-17, 18-64, 65 or above), a partial one-way hash value and the generation timestamp.
