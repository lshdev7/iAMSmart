Security & Privacy Protection

Personal information provided for registration and other information voluntarily provided is encrypted and stored in government data centre facilities.

  • Your “iAM Smart” account is bound to one personal mobile phone.
  • Every use of the 6-digit password triggers a notification email.
  • If an incorrect password is entered ten times in a row, the account becomes invalid and you must register again.
  • Data is shared with an online service only after you give consent in the app.
  • For security reasons, scan “iAM Smart” QR codes from government websites or the app only.
