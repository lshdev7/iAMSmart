“iAM Smart” Mobile App

The “iAM Smart” mobile app is your personal portal to digital government services.

  • Personal Code
  • Digital Document
  • “e-ME” Profile
  • Account Information
  • To-do list
  • Application status
  • Settings

Open “iAM Smart” app

Login

You can log in with biometric authentication (fingerprint or face) by default. You can also log in with your 6-digit password after verifying your email address.

Personal Code

The Personal Code is a QR code which allows you to identify yourself to third parties without presenting your identity card. It contains your partial English name, age group, a one-way hash value and the generation time.

Settings

  • Account information and contact email address
  • Log records of the past 90 days
  • Reset password
  • Transfer account to another mobile phone
  • Automatic logout after a period of inactivity
  • De-register
